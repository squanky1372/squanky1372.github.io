Board Editor

Draw arbitrary piece shapes, arrange them on the board, and export JavaScript.

Piece editor

Toggle squares to draw a shape. The red squares make up one piece. At least one square must be selected.

When enabled, the next placements are black obstacle tiles. They block movement but cannot be moved themselves.

Click an empty board cell to place your drawn shape with that cell as its top-left anchor. Click a placed piece to remove it. Pieces cannot overlap or extend beyond the board.
